Why Pahadi Traveller

We are a small outfit from the hills. Every trip is run with local guides, local kitchens and local homes — so the money stays where the mountains are.

  • Local first. Guides, cooks and stays from the valleys we walk.
  • Small groups. Ten travellers, never more.
  • Leave no trace. We carry our waste back down. All of it.
